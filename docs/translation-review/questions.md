# Open questions for the reviewers

Things I could not settle without a native speaker. Please answer in your review (the *Comment* column works).

## Hindi
1. **मीटिंग or बैठक?** The app says *मीटिंग* everywhere, except the recording notice shown to participants (*इस बैठक की रिकॉर्डिंग हो रही है*), where *बैठक* seemed
   clearer for people who do not use the app. Should one word be used throughout?
2. **ट्रांसक्रिप्ट or प्रतिलेख** for "transcript"? The app uses *ट्रांसक्रिप्ट*.
3. **कार्य** is used for both "action item" and "task". Is that clear, or should action items be *कार्य-बिंदु* / *करने योग्य कार्य*?
4. **Spelled-out English words:** the sample and some labels write *क्लाइंट, स्टैंड-अप, डिलीवरी* in Devanagari. Is that how people actually write them, or would
   you keep some in English letters?

## Tamil
1. **கூட்டம் or சந்திப்பு** for "meeting"? The app uses *கூட்டம்*.
2. **உரைப்பதிவு** (transcript) and **ஒலிப்பதிவு** (audio recording) look alike. Is that confusing in the buttons next to each other?
3. Formal or colloquial? The app uses plain written Tamil; is that right for an office tool?
4. Tamil is marked "experimental" because the speech recognition is weak. Does the wording of that warning read as honest without scaring people off?

## Both
- Is any text too long for a button or tab? Tell us the screen name from the sheet.
- Are the AI-related warnings (consent, "notes can contain mistakes", live-caption privacy) serious enough and clear?
